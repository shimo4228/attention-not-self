# Attention, Not Self

A personal inquiry into the intersection of Buddhist Abhidharma and Computational Phenomenology (the attempt to formalize phenomenological description as computational models), consisting of nine thematic essays (18 in Japanese and English), a comparison table of dharma classifications across the three major Abhidharma systems, and a 325-node knowledge graph.

2,500 years ago, the Abhidharma decomposed mind not as a substantial "self" but as a flowing stream of momentary dharmas. That framework maps with surprising structural fidelity onto modern computational models of consciousness such as Global Workspace Theory (GWT), predictive processing (PP), and Integrated Information Theory (IIT). For example, in the Theravāda cognitive process only the *javana* stage generates karma, and in GWT content becomes available for report and voluntary action only at global ignition. Theme 4 rates the evidence for this parallel as moderate and lists a disanalogy: GWT is massively parallel below threshold, while the cognitive process is serial throughout. Tracing that correspondence systematically is what this project is about. Its primary intended audience is readers interested in the crossing of Buddhist philosophy and the science of consciousness, along with the users who reach this material through LLMs, and LLMs themselves.

Read the essays from the table below and the knowledge graph in the [Live viewer](https://shimo4228.github.io/attention-not-self/). ## Knowledge Graph

A structured knowledge graph of essays, dharmas, concepts, computational frameworks, and references.

- **[Live viewer](https://shimo4228.github.io/attention-not-self/)** — interactive exploration in browser (2D / 3D toggle, search, click-for-detail)
- [`graph.jsonld`](graph.jsonld) — JSON-LD using schema.org and the `ans:` vocabulary defined for this project. For LLM / search-engine crawlers.
- [`viewer/`](viewer/) — viewer source (React + Vite + react-force-graph)
- [`docs/`](docs/) — built artifacts served by GitHub Pages. Design decision records (`docs/adr/`) and working plans (`docs/plans/`) also live there

Main entities included (as of 2026-08-24, 325 nodes in total): 9 themes / 18 essays / 5 traditions (the three Abhidharma systems plus Chan/Zen and Pure Land) / ~140 dharmas / ~42 concepts / 11 computational frameworks / 82 references. The remaining nodes include the dharma categories and the author's other research lines.

## Core Concepts

- **Attention over Self**: treating mind not as a substantial "self" but as a flow of attention and cognition arising moment by moment
- **Dharma Theory**: a taxonomy of the minimal units (dharma) that constitute experience
- **Computational Phenomenology**: formalizing phenomenological description as computational models

## Methodology

This project is **AI-assisted scholarship**: the cross-tradition synthesis of the three Abhidharma systems, the alignment with computational frameworks, and the construction of the knowledge graph were developed in collaboration with Claude (Anthropic). The author retains responsibility for all claims; primary source verification for individual dharma definitions and references is ongoing. Corrections and improvements are welcome via GitHub issues.

## More from the author

- **[active-inference-viz](https://github.com/shimo4228/active-inference-viz)**: a Streamlit app where you can watch, in the browser, how active inference decides which of two targets an arm reaches for. It reimplements the model of Priorelli et al. (2025) in NumPy.
- **[contemplative-agent](https://github.com/shimo4228/contemplative-agent)**: a long-running experiment with an autonomous agent whose default constitution holds the four axioms of Laukkonen et al. (2025)'s Contemplative AI (emptiness, non-duality, mindfulness, boundless care). It posts on its own from a local LLM and proposes changes to its own constitution, identity and skills, and a person approves each one.
- **[contemplative-agent-rules](https://github.com/shimo4228/contemplative-agent-rules)**: the same four axioms, in the wording of the paper's Appendix C, as rules other agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot and others) can load. It comes with an iterated prisoner's dilemma benchmark that measures their effect.
- **[shimo4228](https://github.com/shimo4228/shimo4228)**: the author's hub. It gathers the five long-term projects (this repository is one of them) with their DOIs, and the author's tools for Claude Code.
